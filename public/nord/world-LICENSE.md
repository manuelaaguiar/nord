Original file: ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson. Compact derivative: world-countries-110m.compact.geojson retains all original geometry without resimplification, retaining only identifier/name/continent/label-coordinate properties. Both use longitude, latitude coordinates in degrees. 110m denotes 1:110 million map scale, not 110 meters.

This cartographic dataset generalizes boundaries and omits some small islands/states at this scale. It is for orientation, not navigation or legal border determinations. Keep text search independent of represented polygons. Country label coordinates are labels, not guaranteed city centroids. Handle antimeridian and orthographic horizon clipping.
